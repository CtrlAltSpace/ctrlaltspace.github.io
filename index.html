About

A little about us

Who we are

We are an indie studio operated by a single developer that is a student currently studying high school making projects driven by our own curiosity and creativity.

What we enjoy building

We mostly enjoy building silly stuff, but we do make sure that at the end, there's still an impact you can get from it.

Why we build

We build for the fun of it. But we also build to make an impact to our environment as we believe that we are nothing without the people around us.

How we build

We start every project from scratch, sometimes using AI chatbots for planning. We then build it with help from AI agents when tackling unfamiliar challenges or debugging.
